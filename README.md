# Kevin Durant Shot Chart Analysis

Basketball analytics and data visualization project examining Kevin Durant's shot locations during his historic playoff performance against the Milwaukee Bucks on June 15, 2021.

## Game

**Kevin Durant vs. Milwaukee Bucks — June 15, 2021**

- 49 points
- 17 rebounds
- 10 assists
- 4–9 from three-point range

## Project Overview

This project uses **R** and **ggplot2** to visualize Kevin Durant's made and missed field-goal attempts during one of the most memorable playoff performances of his career.

Rather than looking only at a traditional box score, the shot chart places each field-goal attempt on the basketball court to show the spatial distribution of Durant's offense.

## Shot Chart

![Kevin Durant Shot Chart](images/kevin-durant-shot-chart.png)

The visualization distinguishes:

- Made field goals
- Missed field goals
- Three-point attempts
- Mid-range attempts
- Paint and rim attempts

The resulting shot chart provides a visual representation of how Durant generated his scoring across different areas of the floor.

## Why Shot Charts?

Traditional box scores summarize performance but do not show where scoring opportunities occurred.

Shot-location data can help analyze:

- Shot selection
- Scoring efficiency
- Preferred shooting areas
- Floor spacing
- Three-point volume
- Rim and paint activity
- Mid-range usage

This project demonstrates how basketball data can be transformed into an intuitive visual analytics product.
